Sketching the Derivative

Calculus I  ·  §2.2.7  ·  Christopher Halverson  ·  Fall 2026

How to use this practice The graph of \(f\) is drawn in navy. Use the toolbar to place markers, then sketch where you think \(f'\) should be:
• Key Point (circle) — place where \(f' = 0\) (i.e., where \(f\) has a local max or min)
• Vertex (diamond) — place at the highest or lowest point of the \(f'\) curve itself
Click a placed marker to remove it. Hit Check Sketch to grade your key points.
