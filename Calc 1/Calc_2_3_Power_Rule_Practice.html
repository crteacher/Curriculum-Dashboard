The Power Rule

Calculus I  ·  §2.3  ·  Instructor: Christopher Halverson  ·  Fall 2026

Core Ideas
The Power Rule & Friends $$\frac{d}{dx}\left[x^n\right] = nx^{n-1} \qquad \frac{d}{dx}\left[cf(x)\right] = cf'(x) \qquad \frac{d}{dx}\left[f \pm g\right] = f' \pm g'$$ Apply term by term. Constants differentiate to $0$. Rewrite roots and fractions as powers of $x$ (fractional or negative exponents) before differentiating.
Rewriting Roots & Fractions $$\sqrt{x}=x^{1/2} \qquad \sqrt[3]{x}=x^{1/3} \qquad \frac{1}{x^n}=x^{-n}$$ Rewrite first, then apply the power rule term by term.
Practice Problems

Every problem asks for $f'(x)$. Use ^ for powers, and wrap negative or fractional exponents in parentheses, like x^(-1/2) or -6x^(-3). Example: for f(x)=4x^3, type 12x^2. Press Check or hit Enter.
