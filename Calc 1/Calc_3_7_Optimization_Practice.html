Core Ideas
Optimization Strategy (1) Write the quantity to optimize as a function of one variable. (2) Use a constraint to eliminate the second variable. (3) Set the derivative to $0$ and solve for critical points. (4) Compare critical points AND endpoints to find the true max/min.
Watch the Domain Real-world constraints (lengths must be positive, cuts can't exceed half a side, etc.) often restrict the domain to a closed interval. A critical point outside that domain is not a valid candidate.
