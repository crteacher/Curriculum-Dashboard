Integral Notation & Geometric Area

Calculus I  ·  §4.1.5  ·  Instructor: Christopher Halverson  ·  Fall 2026

Core Ideas
Geometric Rules $\displaystyle\int_a^b c\,f\,dx = c\int_a^b f\,dx$   $\displaystyle\int_a^b(f\pm g)\,dx = \int_a^b f\,dx \pm \int_a^b g\,dx$   $\displaystyle\int_a^b f\,dx + \int_b^c f\,dx = \int_a^c f\,dx$
Signed Area & Symmetry Area below the $x$-axis is negative.  If $f$ is odd ($f(-x)=-f(x)$): $\displaystyle\int_{-a}^{a}f\,dx=0$ — pieces cancel.  If $f$ is even ($f(-x)=f(x)$): $\displaystyle\int_{-a}^{a}f\,dx=2\int_0^a f\,dx$ — halves add.
Shapes to Know Rectangle: $A=\text{width}\times\text{height}$   Right triangle: $A=\tfrac{1}{2}\,b\,h$   Quarter-circle of radius $r$: $A=\dfrac{\pi r^2}{4}$
