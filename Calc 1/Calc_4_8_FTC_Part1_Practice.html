Core Ideas
FTC Part 1 If $f$ is continuous and $G(x)=\int_a^x f(t)\,dt$, then $G'(x)=f(x)$. Chain-rule variant: $\dfrac{d}{dx}\!\left[\int_a^{g(x)}\!f(t)\,dt\right]=f(g(x))\cdot g'(x)$.
What G tells you $G(a)=0$. $G$ increases where $f>0$, decreases where $f<0$, and has a local max or min where $f$ changes sign. $G''=f'$.
