One-Sided Limits

Calculus I  ·  §1.1.5  ·  Instructor: Christopher Halverson  ·  Fall 2026

Core Ideas
DefinitionA one-sided limit $\lim_{x\to a^-}f(x)$ (or $\lim_{x\to a^+}f(x)$) considers approach from only one direction. The two-sided limit exists $\iff$ both one-sided limits exist and are equal.
Reading a graphAn open circle shows the height the curve approaches; a filled dot shows the actual function value. Trace the curve from the left to find the left-hand limit, and from the right to find the right-hand limit.
