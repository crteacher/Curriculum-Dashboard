Related Rates

Calculus I  ·  §3.2  ·  Instructor: Christopher Halverson  ·  Fall 2026

Core Ideas
Related Rates — Setup Two or more quantities are related by an equation, and all are secretly functions of time $t$. (1) Draw a picture and label variables. (2) Write a relating equation. (3) Differentiate implicitly with respect to $t$ — every variable gets a $\dfrac{d(\cdot)}{dt}$. (4) Substitute known values (after differentiating!) and solve for the missing rate.
Common Relating Equations Right triangle: $x^2+y^2=z^2$.   Circle: $A=\pi r^2$, $C=2\pi r$.   Sphere: $V=\dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3$.   Cylinder: $V=\pi r^2h$.   Cube: $V=s^3$.
Practice Problems

All answers are integers (a couple are negative — include the minus sign). When a problem states a rate as a multiple of π, the π is already given to you; your answer is just the coefficient. Press Check or hit Enter.
