Core Ideas
The Definite Integral $$\int_a^b f(x)\,dx = \lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{i=1}^{n}f(x_i)\,\Delta x$$ $\sum i = \tfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$    $\sum i^2 = \tfrac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}$
FTC Part 1  |  Properties If $G(x)=\int_a^x f(t)\,dt$, then $G'(x)=f(x)$.  Chain rule variant: $\dfrac{d}{dx}\!\left[\int_a^{g(x)}\!f(t)\,dt\right]=f(g(x))\cdot g'(x)$.  Key properties: $\int_a^a f=0$;  $\int_a^b f=-\int_b^a f$;  $\int_a^b f=\int_a^c f+\int_c^b f$.
