Inequalities & Interval Notation: A Guided Walkthrough

Builds up the skills behind problems like "−2 < 3x + 1 ≤ 10" and "x < 3 or x > 7" — solving inequalities (and knowing when to flip the sign), writing the answer in interval notation, and combining two conditions with AND or OR.

Work through the stages in order — each one builds on the last. In every stage: watch the short video if a concept feels shaky, try the "What went wrong?" checks first (they're built around the exact mistakes that trip people up), then drill the practice problem — click New version to redo it with new numbers as many times as you want. Starting in Stage 2, you build your answer by tapping directly on a number line instead of typing notation by hand. Stage 5 is a short practice exam to confirm it's sticking.
