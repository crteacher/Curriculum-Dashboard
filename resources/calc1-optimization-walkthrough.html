Optimization: A Guided Walkthrough

A self-paced lesson. You start with a quick critical-point refresher, practice the optimization recipe on a simple problem, then work through four classic word problems: a box, a fenced pen, a cylinder, and the closest point on a curve. Everything you need is on this page.

How today works

You will work through six short stages at your own pace. Plan on about 55 minutes. Keep scratch paper, a pencil, and a calculator next to you. Draw a picture on paper for every word problem.

1 LearnRead the explanation and step through the worked example.
2 PracticeUnscored. Press New version as many times as you like.
3 CheckpointScored, one point per step. Your first check on each step counts.
  1. Each problem is broken into steps. A step unlocks when you get the one before it. Type functions like x(20-x) or 2pir^2 + 700/r, and intervals like (0, 15). Stage 2 explains exactly how.
  2. Hints are free. Opening an answer before you check a checkpoint step scores 0 for that step, so try it first.
  3. The page records your scores and your time on each stage. At the end, go to Summary and take a screenshot of it.
  4. Do not refresh or close this tab until you have turned in your summary. Your progress is not saved if the page reloads.
When you finish

Take a screenshot of your Summary screen and upload it to the Google Classroom assignment immediately after you finish. Do it before you close this tab, because nothing on this page is saved.

To capture just part of the screen, press Ctrl + Shift + Show windows and drag a box. Screenshots are saved in your Downloads folder.
