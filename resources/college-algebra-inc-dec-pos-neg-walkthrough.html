How today works

You will work through seven short stages at your own pace. Plan on about 65 minutes for all seven. Keep scratch paper and a pencil next to you: sketching and marking up the graphs on paper makes every stage easier.

1 LearnRead the explanation and step through the worked examples.
2 PracticeUnscored. Use the hints, new versions, and solutions as much as you want.
3 CheckpointScored. No hints. Your first check is the one that counts.
  1. Answers about intervals are typed with the keyboard, like (-3, 2) U (5, inf). Stage 2 shows exactly how.
  2. If you open a solution before you check a checkpoint, that part scores 0. Try it first.
  3. Your page keeps a record of your scores and your time on each stage. At the end, go to Summary and press Copy summary text.
  4. Do not refresh or close this tab until you have turned in your summary. Your progress is not saved if the page reloads.
  5. Finished early? There is a Bonus stage after Stage 7.
