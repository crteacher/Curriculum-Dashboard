Summation Notation: A Guided Walkthrough

A self-paced lesson on sigma notation, the shorthand for adding up long lists of numbers. You will read it, expand it, write it, shift it, simplify it with rules, and use it to estimate area with rectangles. No limits today. Everything you need is on this page.

How today works

You will work through seven short stages at your own pace, plus an optional bonus stage. Plan on about 65 minutes. Keep scratch paper, a pencil, and a calculator next to you.
