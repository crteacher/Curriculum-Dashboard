Solving Equations: A Guided Walkthrough

Rebuilds the skills underneath "2x − 9 = 3x + 8" — combining like terms, doing the same thing to both sides, and keeping x-terms straight when they show up on both sides of the equals sign.

Work through the stages in order — each one builds on the last. In every stage: watch the short video if a concept feels shaky, try the "What went wrong?" checks first (they're built around the exact mistakes that trip people up), then drill the practice problem — click New version to redo it with new numbers as many times as you want. Stage 6 is a short practice exam to confirm it's sticking.
