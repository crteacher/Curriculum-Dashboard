A review activity: nine short stages on basic antiderivatives and the properties of definite integrals, plus a bonus challenge. Plan on about 50 to 55 minutes. Keep scratch paper and a calculator handy.

How today works

  • Work through the stages in order. Each stage starts with a short reminder, then questions with fresh random numbers.
  • Your first Check on each question is your exam score. After that you can check again for practice, or press New version for new numbers.
  • Opening Show solution before you have checked scores 0 for that question. Try it first.
  • Type answers like x^3/3 + C, sin(x), e^x, ln|x|, 7/2. Any equivalent form is accepted. Your progress is not saved if the page reloads.
