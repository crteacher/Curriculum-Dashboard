Pre-Calculus · §3.4 Polynomial Functions

Polynomial Functions: A Guided Walkthrough

A self-paced lesson on polynomial functions: what makes a function a polynomial, how the zeros and their multiplicities shape the graph, and how the degree and leading coefficient control the ends. You will explore with two interactive graph tools, then practice on random problems. Everything you need is on this page.

How today works

You will work through six short stages at your own pace, plus an optional bonus stage. Plan on about 65 minutes. Keep scratch paper and a pencil next to you.

1 LearnRead the explanation and step through the worked example.
2 PracticeUnscored. Press New version as many times as you like.
3 CheckpointScored, one point per step. Your first check on each step counts.
  1. Each problem is broken into steps. A step unlocks when you get the one before it. Type factors like (x-2)^2(x+1) and lists like -3, 1/2. The “How to type your answers” box on each problem explains how.
  2. Hints are free. Opening an answer before you check a checkpoint step scores 0 for that step, so try it first.
  3. The page records your scores and your time on each stage. At the end, go to Summary and take a screenshot of it.
  4. Do not refresh or close this tab until you have turned in your summary. Your progress is not saved if the page reloads.
