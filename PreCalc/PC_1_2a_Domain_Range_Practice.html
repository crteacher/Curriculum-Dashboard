Domain and Interval Notation

PreCalc  ·  §1.2 Pt 1  ·  Christopher Halverson  ·  Fall 2026

Core Ideas
Interval notation $[a,b]$ includes both endpoints  |  $(a,b)$ excludes both  |  $[a,b)$ includes $a$ only.
Unbounded: $x > 3 \Rightarrow (3,\infty)$.   Union: type (-inf,2)u(2,inf).
Finding the domain Denominator: exclude values where denominator $= 0$.
Even root $\sqrt{\phantom{x}}$: set radicand $\geq 0$ and solve.
$\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{\phantom{x}}}$: set radicand $> 0$ (can't divide by zero).
Domain and range from a graph Domain: the $x$-values the graph covers, read left to right.
Range: the $y$-values the graph covers, read bottom to top.
Practice Problems

Write domains in interval notation, e.g. [3,inf) or (-inf,2)u(2,inf). Press Check or hit Enter.
