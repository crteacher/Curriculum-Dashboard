You need the vertex. Convert to vertex form by completing the square: factor out −16, then add and subtract $(b/2)^2$ inside the parentheses.
Part (c)
When does the object hit the ground? (Round to two decimal places.)
t = s
Set h(t) = 0. Check the discriminant first — is it a perfect square? If not, reach for the quadratic formula. Take the positive root.

t = s
You already found this in part (b). The axis of symmetry passes through the vertex. There is also an algebraic shortcut using $a$ and $b$ from standard form.
