Graph Properties

PreCalc  ·  §1.3  ·  Christopher Halverson  ·  Fall 2026

Core Ideas
Positive & negative intervals A graph is positive where it lies above the x-axis ($f(x)>0$) and negative where it lies below ($f(x)<0$). It crosses zero at each x-intercept — that's the boundary between a positive interval and a negative one.
Increasing, decreasing, extrema Read the graph left to right. Report intervals using open interval notation.
Local max/min: highest/lowest in a neighborhood.
Absolute max/min: highest/lowest over the entire domain (may not exist).
Average rate of change (ARC) $$\text{ARC} = \frac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}$$ This is the slope of the secant line connecting $(a,f(a))$ and $(b,f(b))$.
