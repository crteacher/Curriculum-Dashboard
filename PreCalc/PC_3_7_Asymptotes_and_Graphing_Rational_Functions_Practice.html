Asymptotes & Graphing Rational Functions

PreCalc  ·  §3.7  ·  Christopher Halverson  ·  Fall 2026

Core Ideas
End behavior from the leading terms When $|x|$ is huge only the leading terms matter: $f(x)\approx\dfrac{a x^{n}}{c x^{d}}$.
• $n• $n>d$: no horizontal asymptote; $f\to\pm\infty$, and the sign comes from $\dfrac{a}{c}x^{\,n-d}$ (remember $x^{\,n-d}$ changes sign as $x\to-\infty$ when $n-d$ is odd).
Behavior beside a vertical asymptote Near $x=a$, $f\to+\infty$ or $-\infty$ depending on the sign of $f$ just left and just right of $a$.
• An odd power of $(x-a)$ in the denominator: the two sides go opposite ways.
• An even power: both sides go the same way.
Track the sign of each factor at a test point to decide up or down.
Graphing checklist 1. Cancel common factors (holes). 2. Vertical asymptotes. 3. Horizontal asymptote (if any). 4. Intercepts. 5. Up/down on each side of every vertical asymptote. 6. Connect the pieces. (A graph can cross its horizontal asymptote in the middle.)
