Domain and Range — Graphically

College Algebra  ·  §3.1b  ·  Instructor: Christopher Halverson  ·  Fall 2026

Core Ideas
Domain & Range — Graphically Domain = all x-values where the graph exists (scan left → right).
Range = all y-values the graph reaches (scan bottom ↑ top).
Filled dot ● → include endpoint: [ or ]   Open circle ○ → exclude: ( or )   Arrow → continues to ±∞: write inf or -inf
