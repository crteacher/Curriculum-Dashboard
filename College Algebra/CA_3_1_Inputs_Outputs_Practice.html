Inputs and Outputs

College Algebra  ·  §3.1  ·  Instructor: Christopher Halverson  ·  Fall 2026

Core Ideas
Evaluating (Input → Output) $f(c)$: substitute $x = c$ everywhere in the formula and simplify. The result is the output (y-value); the point $(c,\,f(c))$ is on the graph.
Reversing (Output → Input) Given $f(x) = k$: set the formula equal to $k$ and solve for $x$. This is the same algebra as §1.1 — just with a named function.
