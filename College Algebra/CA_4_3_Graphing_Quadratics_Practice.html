Graphing Quadratics

College Algebra  ·  §4.3  ·  Instructor: Christopher Halverson  ·  Fall 2026

Core Ideas
How to graph a parabola 1. Find the vertex. 2. Decide whether it opens up ($a>0$, vertex is the minimum) or down ($a<0$, vertex is the maximum). 3. Draw the curve from the vertex.
Standard Form f(x) = ax² + bx + c Vertex x-coordinate: $h = -b\,/\,(2a)$, then $k = f(h)$. The y-intercept is $(0, c)$.
Vertex Form f(x) = a(x − h)² + k Vertex $(h, k)$ read directly. Watch the sign: $x+2 = x-(-2)$, so $h=-2$.
Practice Problems

Typed problems: integers (for x=2 enter the whole equation), or max / min. Press Check or hit Enter. Graph problems: click the vertex, then choose Opens up or Opens down. Click a different spot to move the vertex.
