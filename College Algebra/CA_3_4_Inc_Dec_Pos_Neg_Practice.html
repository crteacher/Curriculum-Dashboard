Increasing, Decreasing, Positive & Negative

College Algebra  ·  §3.4  ·  Instructor: Christopher Halverson  ·  Fall 2026

Core Ideas
Reading from a Graph Positive: graph above the x-axis    Negative: graph below the x-axis.
Increasing: graph rises left to right (right of the turning point for a ∪-parabola).
Decreasing: graph falls left to right (left of the turning point).
Navy dots = x-intercepts    Gold dot = vertex (turning point).
Interval Notation Write the x-values where the behavior occurs, not y-values. Use open parentheses at x-intercepts and $\pm\infty$.
Example: positive between $x=-2$ and $x=3$ → $(-2,3)$. Type inf for $\infty$ and -inf for $-\infty$.
Practice Problems

For interval questions type e.g. (-2,inf) or (1,3). For positive/negative and increasing/decreasing questions type the word in full. For x-value questions type just the number. Press Check or hit Enter.
